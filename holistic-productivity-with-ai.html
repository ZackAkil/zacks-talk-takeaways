02 / Cognitive Load

Ref: Oliver Lovell, Cognitive Load Theory in Action. Do not remove all cognitive load—only remove unnecessary friction.

Extrinsic Load [Automate]
Messy UIs, repetitive admin, formatting, finding coupon codes, and navigating complex settings panels.
Intrinsic Load [Protect]
Creativity, critical thinking, empathy, relationship-building, and meaningful human communication.

03 / AI Utility Matrix

Ref: Original hand-drawn AI Utility Matrix post on LinkedIn.

CORE PRINCIPLE: AI is only useful when you can validate the output. Either immedtaly as a domain expert, or eventually as a cynical learner.
High Precision + High Validation

Domain Acceleration

Experts specifying exact requirements and verifying the output (e.g., directing code structure or filtering tour hotel logistics).

Exploratory Prompt + High Validation

Accelerated Learning

Asking AI how and why to do something unfamiliar, then stepping through manually to verify, understand, and internalize the skill.

Low Precision + Low Validation

Slop Land (Full YOLO)

Vague prompts with unreviewed outputs—unmonitored autonomous agents or generic automated customer service loops.

04 / High-Leverage Pattern: Guided Navigation Over Automation

PRIMARY RECOMMENDATION // GEMINI IN CHROME

Ask "How" and "Why" — Never "Do It For Me"

When facing complex admin dashboards, messy settings panels, or unfamiliar software (like Gmail filter setups, YouTube studio panels, or macro keypad software), open Gemini in Chrome so it can see the current page context.

Do not let AI click buttons for you. UIs are brittle and blind automation hides mistakes. Instead, ask AI to guide you step-by-step and explain why each setting matters. Walking through the steps yourself lets you catch hallucinations immediately and permanently levels up your own skill.

05 / Practical Stack & Workflows

Inbox Zero Setup
Configure Gmail (or Outlook) with a multiple-inbox split view built around three triage labels: To Do, Waiting, and Read Through (see Jeff Su's Inbox Zero guide). Open Gemini in Chrome while in your inbox settings to guide you through the setup step-by-step and help create automatic Gmail filters to route newsletters and recurring noise out of your primary inbox.
Google Tasks
Executive to-do listing. Keep a strict Today list that hits zero every evening, and move deferred tasks to a separate Kick the Can list so your workday is intentionally finished.
Macro Keys
Small programmable hardware keypads you can map to any repetitive workflow or multi-step shortcut in your day-to-day tools (for example, mapping a 3-key pad to your inbox triage labels for single-keystroke sorting). Use AI to guide you through the keypad's complex configuration software.
[Advanced] Google Apps Script
Use AI to help write a script that mirrors speaking or travel events as color-coded full-day calendar banners for instant monthly visibility.
